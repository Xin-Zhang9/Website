About

Science understood deeply, then communicated clearly.

I am a doctoral researcher at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. My work combines engineering, cellular biology, quantitative imaging, bioelectronic devices, and data analysis.

Alongside research, I am developing a practice in scientific illustration, 3D visualization, and medical animation. I am especially interested in work that helps researchers, clinicians, and wider audiences understand complex biological processes.
